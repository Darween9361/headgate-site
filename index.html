Our specialty

Why our MLV vaccines come freeze-dried

Modified-live viral vaccines (IBR, BVD, PI3, BRSV) are fragile. Lyophilizing — freeze-drying the virus into a dry cake inside the vial — is how we keep the dose you give in June as potent as the day it left the plant.

At the plant

Frozen, then dried

Live virus is frozen and the water removed under vacuum, leaving a stable cake with potency locked in.

In your fridge

Dry cake + sterile diluent

Two vials: the freeze-dried vaccine and its diluent. Keep both at 35–45°F; the cake is far more forgiving of a warm truck than liquid MLV.

At the chute

Mix, swirl, use within an hour

Transfer diluent into the cake, swirl gently — never shake — and keep it on ice. Mix only what you'll run through the chute in the next hour.

Potency

Freeze-drying stops the degradation that liquid live-virus vaccines suffer from the moment they're filled. The dose on the label is the dose in the syringe.

Shelf life

Unopened lyophilized MLV carries a longer dating than liquid equivalents, so you can buy for the season without watching the expiration.

Cold-chain margin

Every vaccine must stay refrigerated, but a dry cake tolerates the real-world minutes on a tailgate or in a cooler far better than a reconstituted dose.

Combo flexibility

Our 5-Way + VL5 uses the liquid Vibrio-Lepto bacterin as the diluent — one reconstitution, one shot, full pre-breeding coverage.

Built for ranch work

Every vial is sized for a chute day: 10-dose for small groups, 50-dose for the big run, with a tear-off cap that opens with gloves on.
